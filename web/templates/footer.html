cookie Gingerbread

Converts vector artwork into KiCad PCB files, entirely in your browser — the geometry work is Zig and C/C++ compiled to WebAssembly, so no design ever leaves your machine.

With thanks to Winterbloom and Thea Flowers, who made this wonderful tool and released it for everyone to build on. This is a fork of the original; the upstream version lives at gingerbread.wntr.dev.

Maintained by Carlos Eduardo de Paula of Voltage Foundry Modular.
